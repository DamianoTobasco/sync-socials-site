Schedule social media posts with ChatGPT

Sync Socials is published as an app in ChatGPT. Connect it once, and you can ask ChatGPT to draft posts, schedule the ones you approve, and check whether they went out. You stay in the chat. The posts publish through the social accounts you have already connected.

What ChatGPT can do in your Sync Socials workspace

  • Show your workspace, connected accounts and scheduled posts
  • Save drafts, with or without a destination account
  • Use images and video already in your media library
  • Schedule an approved post for the date, time and timezone you name
  • Publish now, only when you ask it to
  • Edit drafts, reschedule planned posts, or cancel a scheduled post before it goes out
  • Check the delivery result after a post goes out

ChatGPT works with accounts you have already connected in Sync Socials. Connecting ChatGPT does not connect your social accounts for you. You add those in Connections.

What you need

  • A ChatGPT account that can use apps. Your organization may control which apps are available.
  • A Sync Socials account. A free account can connect ChatGPT. No trial, card or API key needed to connect.
  • The Owner role in the workspace you want to connect.
  • Connected social accounts before you schedule or publish. You can save an untargeted draft first.

New to Sync Socials? Create your free workspace, then connect ChatGPT.

Connect ChatGPT to Sync Socials

  1. Open the app. Open Sync Socials in ChatGPT and choose Connect.
  2. Sign in. Sign in to Sync Socials with your workspace-owner account.
  3. Approve access. On the consent screen, check the workspace, email, requested access and return destination. Choose Allow access.
  4. Check the connection. Back in ChatGPT, ask it to show your Sync Socials workspace and connected accounts.

There is no API key to generate and no credentials to paste into a chat. Account linking uses a separate authorization. Availability can depend on your ChatGPT account and organization settings.

Start with a draft

Ask ChatGPT to look first and change nothing:

Show my Sync Socials workspace, connected accounts, and scheduled posts. Do not change anything.

Then save a caption for review:

Save this caption as an untargeted Sync Socials draft: “A small update that makes the everyday easier.” Name it “Product update draft.” Do not schedule or publish it.

Neither prompt schedules or publishes anything.

Adding media

Pick an asset already in your media library, upload through the dashboard, or give ChatGPT a downloadable HTTPS URL you are allowed to use. Files you attach in a ChatGPT conversation are not added to your Sync Socials media library automatically.

Concept and media-generation features may need an AI provider configured in your workspace, billed separately by that provider. Generation can run asynchronously; check the saved post for completion before scheduling it. See Content Studio and your own AI key.

Schedule a post from ChatGPT

When you are ready, name the exact post, the destination accounts, the date, the time and the timezone. Include any privacy or disclosure choice the platform asks for. See how scheduling and publishing work and how timezones are applied.

Schedule my approved “Product update draft” to the Facebook Page I selected on October 15, 2026, at 2:30 p.m. America/New_York. Keep the caption and media unchanged. Show me the saved schedule.

To publish now, ask ChatGPT to publish the selected post to the accounts you name. Saving a draft never publishes it.

After it is scheduled

Check the delivery result. Queued or processing does not mean a post is live. You can reschedule or cancel a scheduled post before delivery. Canceling a Sync Socials post does not remove a post that has already gone out on a social network.

You stay in control

  • Nothing publishes unless you ask for it.
  • Sync Socials does not ask for your social-network passwords. Accounts connect through official OAuth where supported. Telegram uses a bot token, and Discord and Slack use channel webhooks.
  • To disconnect, open Settings → Connected agents and disconnect ChatGPT, or remove the app from ChatGPT.
  • Existing drafts and schedules stay after you disconnect. Cancel unwanted schedules separately. This does not disconnect your social accounts or a Claude authorization.

For the workspace you approve, ChatGPT can read workspace, brand, account, media and post information, and create or edit content. Content returned by the app is available to ChatGPT for your request. See the Sync Socials Privacy Policy. ChatGPT's settings and policies also apply.

Connection help

If connection is unavailable, check your ChatGPT account or organization app settings. Only the workspace owner can connect ChatGPT, so sign in with that account. If the consent screen says the workspace's subscription has ended, renew it in Billing, then connect again.

For an expired authorization, start a fresh connection from the app listing. If a destination needs reconnection, reconnect that social account in Sync Socials before trying to publish again. Verify the destination before retrying a post whose outcome is uncertain.

Contact Sync Socials support with the error and affected workflow. Do not include passwords, API keys, OAuth secrets, or access tokens.

FAQ

Can I schedule social media posts with ChatGPT?

Yes. Connect the Sync Socials app in ChatGPT, then name the post, the accounts, the date, the time and the timezone. Drafting is free. Scheduling and publishing start with the 7-day free trial.

Do I need an API key to connect ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT connects through account authorization. Sign in as your workspace owner and choose Allow access.

Is it free to connect ChatGPT to Sync Socials?

Yes. A free account can connect ChatGPT, show your workspace and accounts, and save drafts. Scheduling and publishing start with the 7-day trial, which needs a card, costs $0 today, then $19.95/month unless you cancel. Your ChatGPT plan is separate.

Which social platforms can ChatGPT post to?

Facebook Pages, linked Instagram professional accounts, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn personal profiles, Bluesky, Telegram, Discord and Slack, plus X/Twitter on an active paid Growth plan. Threads is available in workspaces where Threads access is enabled. Pinterest and LinkedIn company pages are not supported.

Will ChatGPT post without my approval?

Saving a draft never publishes. Scheduling and publishing happen when you ask for them. You can disconnect ChatGPT any time in Settings → Connected agents.

Will posts scheduled through ChatGPT get less reach?

Sync Socials publishes through each platform's API. Reach depends on the platform, content, audience and timing, so we can't promise results identical to a manual post.
