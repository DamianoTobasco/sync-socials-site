Composer

Create, schedule, and publish a post

Write once, choose destinations, pick a time. Sync Socials adapts the post to each platform's format and tells you exactly what was delivered.

Text, image, or video

Start by picking the kind of post. The composer only offers accounts that can take that type: Instagram and TikTok need media, YouTube needs video, LinkedIn takes text or one image, and so on. Switching type clears incompatible selections.

Quick post and all options

The composer opens in Quick post: title, caption, media, destinations, and a time. Choose Show all options for:

  • Per-destination copy: a different caption, or a different title for YouTube and TikTok, for any selected account. Blank fields fall back to the shared caption.
  • Tags: appended as hashtags where the platform supports them.
  • Custom thumbnail for videos on YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram.

Your choice is remembered on that browser. A post that already uses these options always opens with everything shown.

Destinations

Select the connected accounts the post should go to. Remember saves the current selection as your default for next time. Platform limits for the selected accounts, such as image counts and caption lengths, appear under the destination list.

Scheduling

Enter a date and time, or use the suggested slots. Times are read in the workspace timezone shown beside the field; change it in Settings if it's wrong. Schedule queues the post; Publish now sends it immediately; Save draft keeps it without a time.

Scheduled posts show on the Calendar. Drag one to a new day or time to reschedule it without rebuilding the post. Edit a scheduled post from Home or Drafts until it starts publishing.

After publishing

A background worker publishes each destination separately and retries temporary platform errors. Posted lists every post with the result per destination: delivered, failed, or still processing on the platform's side. If one destination fails while others succeed, the post is marked as a partial failure and you can retry just the failed ones.

Unsaved work

If you leave the composer with unsaved changes, Sync Socials keeps a copy on that browser and offers to restore it next time you open the composer for the same post. Files you selected from your device need to be chosen again.
