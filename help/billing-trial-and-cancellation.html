Pricing, the free trial, and cancelling

Start free, upgrade when you need unlimited posting or a team, and cancel from the billing portal whenever you like.

The Free plan

Every new account starts on the Free plan with no card: connect one account per platform (X excluded), upload media, keep unlimited drafts, and schedule or publish your first 3 posts. A post counts once it leaves Drafts, whether or not delivery succeeds; the Home page banner shows how many you have left.

After the third post, scheduling or publishing saves the post as a draft and offers the trial. API access and the Content Studio need a paid plan.

The 7-day trial

When you're ready for unlimited posting, start the trial from the banner or from Billing. Stripe takes a card, charges $0 today, and the subscription renews when the trial ends unless you cancel first: $19.95 per month or $199.50 per year on Growth, $39.99 per month or $399 per year on Agency. X publishing isn't included during the trial.

The trial is for your first paid plan only; Stripe confirms eligibility and the first payment date on the checkout page.

Growth and Agency

Growth includes:

  • Unlimited scheduling and drafts, with no per-post fees.
  • Up to 20 connected accounts per platform.
  • Every live platform, plus the X allowance after the trial.
  • Media library, calendar, analytics for Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube, and delivery tracking for everything.
  • MCP and API access with one active API key.

Agency adds everything a team needs:

  • Invite up to 4 team members (5 seats) to work in the same workspace.
  • Up to 50 connected accounts per platform.
  • Three active API keys, so each tool or agent gets its own.
  • Priority support from the founder.

If Agency isn't offered on your Billing page yet, it's still rolling out; email support and we'll set it up for you.

Change plan, update card, or cancel

The workspace owner opens Billing and chooses Open billing portal. The Stripe portal handles payment methods, invoices, switching between monthly and yearly, and cancellation. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period; you keep access until then.

After a subscription ends

The workspace locks: Billing, Settings, and Referrals stay open, and your drafts, media, and history are kept, but scheduling and publishing stop and queued posts aren't sent. Subscribing again from Billing unlocks everything as it was.

Refunds and billing questions

Email contactsyncsocials@gmail.com from the account email with your workspace name. Refund requests are reviewed by a person; the billing portal and your Stripe receipts are the record of what was charged.
