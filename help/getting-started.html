Getting started with Sync Socials

Three steps take most people under ten minutes. Nothing is charged until you schedule or publish your first post.

Create your free accountConfirm your timezoneConnect your first accountSave a draftSchedule or publish

Create your free account

Go to app.sync-socials.com and sign up with Google or an email address and password. Email signups receive a confirmation link; open it to land in your workspace. You start free with no card: one account per platform and unlimited drafts. Scheduling and publishing start the 7-day free trial.

Every account gets its own workspace. Connected social accounts, drafts, media, and billing belong to the workspace, not to your personal login.

Confirm your timezone

The getting-started card on your Home page asks you to confirm the scheduling timezone. Every time you type into the scheduler is read in this timezone, so set it to where you actually are. You can change it later in Settings; existing scheduled posts keep their original times.

Connect your first account

Open Connections and pick a channel. Facebook, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and X use the platform's own sign-in; Bluesky, Telegram, Discord, and Slack take an app password, bot token, or webhook URL you paste in. Sync Socials never asks for a social-network password.

  • Facebook Pages and Instagram
  • YouTube
  • TikTok
  • LinkedIn
  • Threads and Bluesky
  • X
  • Telegram, Discord, and Slack

Save a draft

Select Create post. The quick composer asks for a title, a caption, media if you want it, and the accounts it should go to. Save it as a draft to come back later, or move straight on to scheduling. Drafts are free and unlimited.

Need a different caption per channel, tags, or a custom video thumbnail? Choose Show all options inside the composer.

Schedule or publish

Pick a date and time, then Schedule, or choose Publish now. The first time you do, Sync Socials saves the post as a draft and asks you to start the 7-day free trial through Stripe: $0 today, then $19.95 per month unless you cancel before it ends. From then on, scheduling is unlimited.

Scheduled posts appear on the Calendar, where you can drag them to a better slot. Delivery results for every destination show up on Posted.
