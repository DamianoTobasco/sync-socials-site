Media

Media requirements by platform

Sync Socials checks media against each selected platform before anything is sent, and converts what it can. These are the rules it applies.

UploadsImages per postVideoCaptions

Uploads

  • Up to 10 files per upload, 25 MB per image, 250 MB per video, and 250 MB per upload in total.
  • Images: JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF. Videos: MP4, M4V, MOV. MOV and other QuickTime files are converted to H.264 MP4 automatically.
  • Large videos are accepted first and normalized in the background; the post shows as processing until that finishes.
  • Everything you upload lands in the Media library and can be reused in later posts. Media attached to a draft or scheduled post can't be deleted until the post no longer needs it.

Images per post

  • Facebook and Instagram: up to 10 images.
  • X: up to 4 images, no animated GIFs.
  • LinkedIn, Telegram, Discord, Slack: 1 image. The composer refuses a multi-image post to these instead of dropping images.
  • TikTok: photo posts are resized to fit TikTok's 1080p bounds.

Instagram requires images between 4:5 and 1.91:1. The composer flags images outside that range and lets you choose a crop focus; the crop is applied only to the Instagram copy.

Video

  • YouTube: video only. Title from the campaign title, description from the caption, optional custom thumbnail.
  • Instagram and Facebook: videos publish as Reels when they meet the platform's rules; otherwise Facebook publishes a regular video post.
  • X: one video up to 140 seconds and 50 MB.
  • TikTok: one video; TikTok finishes processing after upload.
  • LinkedIn: video isn't supported yet.

Captions

  • X: 280 weighted characters including hashtags; links and emoji count differently.
  • Other platforms' caption limits are shown in the composer under the destination list when the selected account needs them.
