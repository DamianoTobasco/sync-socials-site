Content Studio

Content Studio and your own AI key

AI generation is optional. Everything else in Sync Socials works without a key; add one when you want the Studio to draft ideas and videos for you.

What the Studio doesAdd your keyWhat it costsStudio through your agent

What the Studio does

  • Turns a short business brief or website into campaign concepts, each with a hook, a caption, and hashtags.
  • Produces vertical videos from a chosen concept using the built-in reaction-clip library or footage you upload. Rendering from library clips is included at no extra cost.
  • Saves the results as drafts. Nothing is scheduled or published until you approve it.

Add your key

Open Settings → AI keys and paste an OpenAI or Claude API key. Keys are encrypted and used only for your workspace's requests. Usage is billed by your provider at their rates; Sync Socials charges nothing on top.

Optional video generation uses Runway with your own Runway credits. Without it, video concepts keep the script and shot brief in the draft and render from library clips instead.

What it costs

Concept generation uses a small amount of text model usage per run. Image generation and provider-rendered video cost whatever your provider charges; check your provider dashboard for exact pricing. Reaction-clip renders don't use your key.

Studio through your agent

Connected agents can call the same concept and video tools. A workspace AI key is still required for generation; it isn't needed to save a caption, browse media, or schedule.
