LinkedIn

Connect LinkedIn

Sync Socials publishes to LinkedIn personal profiles. Keep the scope in mind before planning a LinkedIn-heavy calendar.

ConnectWhat works and what doesn't

Connect

  1. Open Connections and choose Connect LinkedIn.
  2. Sign in and allow the requested permissions.

LinkedIn tokens last about two months. When one expires, the account shows Needs reconnect; choose Reconnect and your scheduled posts carry on.

What works and what doesn't

  • Supported: text posts and posts with one image, to your personal profile.
  • Not supported yet: company Pages, video, documents, and multi-image galleries. The composer blocks a multi-image post to LinkedIn instead of silently dropping images.

Audience metrics for LinkedIn aren't available in Analytics; delivery results are on Posted.
