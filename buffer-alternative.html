The honest take

When each tool makes sense.

Buffer is a fine choice if you only run one to three channels and want a free plan, or if LinkedIn or Pinterest is your primary network today. It is a solid, well known scheduler and the free tier is genuinely useful for tiny workloads.

Sync Socials is a fit when you want flat pricing, a video Content Studio and publishing to Discord, Slack and Telegram alongside supported social platforms. Both tools offer agent access; compare the destinations and creation workflow you need.

FAQ

Switching questions, answered.

Is Sync Socials cheaper than Buffer?

It depends on your channel count and billing period. Buffer Essentials starts at $5 per channel per month billed yearly, with volume pricing for larger setups. Sync Socials is $19.95 per month or $199.50 per year for up to 20 accounts per platform. Compare your actual setup before choosing.

Can I try Sync Socials before paying?

Yes. Every new account starts with a free 7 day trial of the full product. Cancel anytime in one click.

How do I switch from Buffer to Sync Socials?

Connect supported social accounts through OAuth, or configure a bot token or webhook for community channels. Then reschedule your upcoming posts, or let the Content Studio generate a fresh calendar of content from your website link.

Does Sync Socials support LinkedIn or Pinterest?

LinkedIn personal profiles are supported. Pinterest and LinkedIn company pages are not currently available. TikTok, YouTube, Discord, Slack and Telegram are also live. Facebook Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts are also live; X is coming soon.

What makes Sync Socials different from Buffer?

Sync Socials combines flat subscription pricing, a video Content Studio and publishing to community channels such as Discord, Slack and Telegram. Both products offer AI assistance and agent access through MCP/API, so compare supported destinations and the content workflow you need.
