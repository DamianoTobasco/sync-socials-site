The honest take

When each tool makes sense.

Buffer is a fine choice if you only run one to three channels and want a free plan, or if LinkedIn or Pinterest is your primary network today. It is a solid, well known scheduler and the free tier is genuinely useful for tiny workloads.

Sync Socials wins when you post across several platforms and want the total bill to stay flat, when you want AI to generate the content and not just the caption, and when you want your AI agent to run the whole pipeline instead of you clicking through a dashboard. Buffer's price scales with every channel you add. Ours does not.

FAQ

Switching questions, answered.

Is Sync Socials cheaper than Buffer?

For more than three channels, usually yes. Buffer's Essentials plan is $6 per channel per month, so ten channels costs about $60 a month. Sync Socials is $19.95 a month flat with up to 20 accounts per platform and unlimited scheduled posts.

Can I try Sync Socials before paying?

Yes. Every new account starts with a free 7 day trial of the full product. Cancel anytime in one click.

How do I switch from Buffer to Sync Socials?

Connect your accounts through each platform's official OAuth login, which takes a few minutes. Then reschedule your upcoming posts, or let the Content Studio generate a fresh calendar of content from your website link.

Does Sync Socials support LinkedIn or Pinterest?

LinkedIn personal profiles are supported. Pinterest and LinkedIn company pages are not currently available. TikTok, YouTube, Discord, Slack and Telegram are also live. Facebook and Instagram access for new customers still requires Meta approval; X is coming soon.

What makes Sync Socials different from Buffer?

Three things: one flat price instead of per channel billing, a video Content Studio that turns a business brief and footage into short form videos, and full MCP access so AI agents like Claude Code can run your entire posting pipeline.
