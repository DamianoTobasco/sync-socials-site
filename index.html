FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I use an AI agent with Sync Socials?

Yes. Connect the published ChatGPT app or Claude plugin, or use the hosted MCP/API with Codex, Claude Code, OpenClaw and compatible clients. A Sync Socials workspace with API access is required. ChatGPT and Claude Chat or Cowork use account authorization; API-key clients use a workspace API key. ChatGPT setup, Claude setup, and API-key setup.

Why switch from Buffer or Hootsuite?

Two reasons: price and people. Sync Socials costs a fraction of what the big tools charge, the interface is built for a 30-second posting workflow, and when you need help you talk to the founder directly - not a corporate ticket queue.

What platforms are supported?

TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn personal profiles, Discord, Slack and Telegram are live. Facebook Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts are available for new and existing customers. X/Twitter is live on active paid Growth subscriptions after the trial, with usage limits. Connect X through OAuth in Connections. Pinterest and LinkedIn company pages are not currently supported.

How many posts can I schedule each month?

Scheduling is unlimited across up to 20 accounts per platform, with no per-post fees. X publishing has a separate allowance on active paid Growth: up to 50 attempts per workspace per UTC calendar month, including up to 3 link-post attempts. Daily and per-account limits also apply.

What are the X posting limits?

X requires an active paid Growth subscription after the trial and an X account connected through OAuth. Each workspace gets up to 50 publish attempts per UTC calendar month, including up to 3 with links, and up to 10 per UTC day. Each X account gets up to 30 attempts per month and 3 per day. Failed attempts count, reconnecting does not reset usage, and media can use the included allowance before the post limit is reached. Check your remaining allowance in Connections, Create, or Billing.

Will my posts get less reach by scheduling them?

Sync Socials publishes through each supported platform's API. Reach and engagement depend on the platform, content, audience, and timing, so identical performance to a manual post cannot be guaranteed.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in one click, no questions asked. No contracts and no cancellation hoops.

Do I have to share my social passwords?

Sync Socials does not ask for your social-network passwords. Social accounts use official OAuth where supported; Telegram uses a bot token, and Discord and Slack use channel webhooks.
