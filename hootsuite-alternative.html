The honest take

When each tool makes sense.

Hootsuite earns its price if you are a large team that needs social listening, approval chains, many seats and compliance features. Agencies and enterprises with those requirements are its real audience, and it serves them well.

Substantially. Hootsuite's entry plan starts around $99 a month billed annually. Sync Socials is $19.95 a month, or $16.63 a month billed yearly, which is roughly a fifth of the price, with unlimited scheduled posts included.

Full MCP access so AI agents can run your posting pipeline, a video Content Studio that turns a business brief and footage into short form videos, publishing to community channels like Discord, Slack and Telegram, and month to month billing you can cancel in one click.

What does Hootsuite have that Sync Socials does not?

How do I switch from Hootsuite to Sync Socials?
